Codebases we could use for testing
===================================

1. TheAlgorithms/C-Sharp - https://github.com/TheAlgorithms/C-Sharp
   Sorting, searching, graph algos. Lots of arrays and loops, good for out of bounds testing.

2. FluentValidation - https://github.com/FluentValidation/FluentValidation
   Validation library. Good for null ref and exception testing.

3. dotnet/samples - https://github.com/dotnet/samples
   Official .NET console apps. Good for checking our instrumenter works on real projects.
